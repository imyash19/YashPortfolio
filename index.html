I'm Yash, a data analyst working across analytics, cloud administration and consulting. I spend my days in SQL and Python cleaning up datasets no one wants to touch, then rebuilding them into Power BI and Tableau dashboards that stakeholders actually open.

Simulation work with Deloitte and Accenture sharpened how I approach KPI reporting, stakeholder communication and data quality at an enterprise scale. I hold a BSc in Information Science from Chaudhary Charan Singh University.
